# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and focus on the work. Harassment,
discrimination, and personal attacks are not tolerated in any project space, including issues,
pull requests, discussions, and chat.

To report unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainers privately through the repository's
security/contact channel. Reports will be handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content
or restrict participation for violations.
